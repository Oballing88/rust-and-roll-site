Questions We Get Every Week

Straight answers — the same ones we give over text.

How much does junk removal cost in North County?

$75 minimum, and most single large items (fridge, couch, washer) run $75–$100. A full trailer load or cleanout is $450–$550, dump fees included. Text a photo to (760) 410-6887 and you'll have a flat price within the hour.

Can you take a junk car if I lost the title?

Usually, yes. If you're the registered owner and there's no lienholder on record, California's REG 227 form replaces a lost title — no notary needed, and we'll walk you through it. If you're not the registered owner and there's no title, nobody can legally junk that car, so we'd have to pass.

Is junk car removal really free?

Often, yes — on most complete cars the scrap value covers the tow, so you pay nothing. If the catalytic converter or wheels are missing, or it's a tough spot to pull from, it can run up to $150. Either way you get the number before we hook up. One tip: file the free DMV Release of Liability (REG 138) within 5 days of pickup — it protects you from future tickets on the car.

What areas do you serve?

Vista, Oceanside, Carlsbad, Escondido, Encinitas, and San Marcos — plus most of surrounding North San Diego County. A little outside that? Text us anyway; short trips usually work.

Is there anything you won't haul?

Hazardous waste — paint, chemicals, asbestos — is a no. Appliances, e-waste, scrap metal, furniture, yard debris, and non-running cars are all fair game. Heavy material like concrete or tile gets quoted separately by weight.

How fast can you get here?

Same week, often same day. We answer texts within the hour, 6am–7pm, seven days a week. A photo of the pile speeds up your quote.
